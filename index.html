📋 Room Rules & Guidelines

🤝 General Conduct

  • Be respectful and kind to all community members
  • Use appropriate language suitable for all ages
  • Help create a welcoming environment for new trainers
  • Respect different skill levels and play styles

⚔️ Battle Guidelines

  • No timer stalling or excessive delays
  • Follow agreed-upon battle formats and rules
  • Good sportsmanship in victory and defeat
  • No hacked or illegal Pokemon in competitive play

🚫 Prohibited Actions

  • Spamming messages or excessive caps usage
  • Advertising other servers or unrelated content
  • Harassment, discrimination, or toxic behavior
  • Sharing inappropriate content or links

⚖️ Rule Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, temporary mutes, or permanent bans depending on severity. Our moderators are here to help maintain a positive environment for everyone.
